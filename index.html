Passive radar signal processing for ice sheet monitoring Ongoing

Passive radar uses signals already in the environment instead of a dedicated transmitter, which keeps instruments small but leaves the data noisy and band-limited. I'm developing processing methods that improve signal-to-noise ratio, widen usable bandwidth, and remove the instrument's own response so subsurface echoes stand out.

  • Completed spectral stitching to combine multiple frequency bands into one wider effective bandwidth.
  • Building an automated regularization (λ) optimization for noise reduction in power spectral density estimation, tested on field data from Dumont Dunes.
  • Goal: suppress a system artifact near 0.10 µs while sharpening the true echo near 0.22 µs, then apply the method to higher-elevation and Iceland field data.
  • MATLAB
  • Autocorrelation
  • PSD estimation
  • Regularization
  • Firn aquifers

Empathy and perspective-taking in engineering education

Engineers make decisions that land on people they never meet. This project asks how we can teach students to take those people's perspectives seriously as part of the technical work.

  • Wrote an annotated literature review on empathy in engineering.
  • Designed two workshops that treat empathy as a core skill in humanitarian design, using scenario-based activities on the social and technical consequences of engineering decisions.
  • Built Workshop 2 around the Suncor refinery in Commerce City, Colorado, with stakeholder perspective cards and a "consequence ladder" activity. The second workshop, for mechanical engineering students, explores VR-based perspective-taking and compares mixed versus separated stakeholder groups.
  • Ran a thematic analysis of student interviews on the GEEN 1400 industry mentor program using Braun and Clarke's six-phase framework.
