Aug 2026 – present Undergraduate Researcher Independent study under Dr. Sean Peters ARSENL Lab, CU Boulder

Passive radar signal processing Ongoing

  • Implement spectral stitching in MATLAB to expand passive radar bandwidth for subsurface ice and firn aquifer detection, then apply Noise Level Subtraction (NLS) to suppress the artificial peaks stitching introduces.
  • Tuned the NLS scaling factor to λ = 0.62 on Dumont Dunes data, removing a 100 ns stitching artifact while preserving the 220 ns subsurface echo (verified via PSD).
  • Automating λ optimization for Iceland field data.

Summer 2026 Personal project

Arduino-sequenced analog instrument

  • An Arduino Nano Every keeps the tempo, sends trigger pulses to a kick drum circuit, and sends pitch values to a DAC that turns them into a control voltage.
  • A voltage-controlled oscillator turns that voltage into a note: more voltage, higher pitch.
  • Drew the schematics, learned each chip's pinout, and probed pin by pin. When it first played, every note was out of tune, so I tuned each one by hand.

Circuits inspired by Moritz Klein's tutorials.
